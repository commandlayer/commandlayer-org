The product

Sell outcomes to machines, not another dashboard to humans.

General-purpose agents can reason. They still need external systems that can retrieve data, change state, transact, monitor, verify, and produce evidence. CommandLayer is being built for those service boundaries.

O

Outcome-first

Useful work is packaged behind stable machine contracts instead of exposing raw vendor complexity to every buyer.

$

Commercial by design

Quotes, metering, payment adapters, retries, COGS, and contribution are part of the execution path—not an afterthought.

✓

Execution evidence

Receipts prove what was signed and observed during execution. They do not pretend to certify factual truth.

One factory

Reusable primitives compose higher-value services.

CommandLayer does not need a separate codebase for every service name. Shared primitives, provider adapters, routing, state, metering, receipts, and telemetry sit underneath a small number of evidence-selected storefronts.

Distribution plane

One service definition. Multiple discovery surfaces.

The service manifest is authoritative. HTTP/JSON is canonical. OpenAPI, MCP, ENS, x402, ERC-8004 and other ecosystems are adapters around the service—not separate sources of truth.
