Service catalog

Useful machine services, not a wall of endpoints.

CommandLayer is building a small set of commercially useful outcomes on top of shared primitives. The first paid services are selected by evidence and acceptance contracts—not by domain inventory or developer preference.

Catalog policy

A name is inventory until evidence turns it into a product.

Candidate service identities are deliberately separated from live service claims. A service becomes Live only after its problem, acceptance contract, execution path, economics, and distribution surface are validated.

Permissioned actions

High-consequence actions stay gated.

Names such as purchase, sign, refund, payout, authorize, KYC, and other state-changing services are not launch shortcuts. They require authorization scopes, spend limits, replay safety, recovery paths, and service-specific compliance before they can become public products.
