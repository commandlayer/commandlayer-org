Monitoring, Tracking & State

Observe external state over time and preserve what changed, when and from where.

2First Ten services assigned

Long-lived agent workflows need durable state, not just stateless requests. This family stores observations and snapshots separately from failures so a bad fetch cannot overwrite the last known good state.

What machines buy here

Outcome contracts, not a list of endpoints.

Declare a target, observation/tracking specification and cadence or trigger. Successful observations are hashed and linked to prior state; failed observations are recorded separately and never masquerade as no-change.
