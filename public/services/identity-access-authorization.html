Identity, Access & Authorization

Bind who or what may act to explicit machine-readable scopes.

0First Ten services assigned

Identity answers who a machine or service claims to be; authorization answers what it may do. CommandLayer keeps these separate from settlement and execution evidence so permission is explicit rather than inferred from possession of a wallet or API key.
