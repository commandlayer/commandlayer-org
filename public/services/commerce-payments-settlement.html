Commerce, Payments & Settlement

Let machines obtain a price, settle and invoke without a human checkout flow.

0First Ten services assigned

Machine commerce needs price discovery, bounded payment, idempotent settlement and atomic admission. CommandLayer treats x402 and other payment systems as settlement adapters around the service contract—not as the product itself.

What machines buy here

Outcome contracts, not a list of endpoints.

A buyer requests a priced service, receives payment requirements when settlement is required, pays on an allowed rail and retries with payment evidence. Admission remains idempotent and service-policy aware.
