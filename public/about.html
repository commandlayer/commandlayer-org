About CommandLayer

Build the things agents spend money on.

Model routers, wallets, payment rails, registries, and general-purpose agents are becoming infrastructure. CommandLayer is focused on the other side of that market: useful machine services those agents can actually buy.

Positioning

Seller-side infrastructure for machine commerce.

↔

Not a model router

Routing is cost, quality, and reliability infrastructure. It can use multiple providers without becoming the company's moat.

◇

Not a payment protocol

Settlement rails are adapters. Metering, pricing, execution economics, and outcome quality remain independent of one rail.

◎

Not a generic registry

Directories and identity systems help buyers find a service. They do not replace the service, its contract, or its execution evidence.

What compounds

Composition, state, and execution evidence.

The factory is designed around three durable sources of value: combining primitives into better outcomes, maintaining state over time, and producing evidence about execution or observed state that a third party can inspect later.

M1 · Composition

Turn several providers and primitives into one accepted result.

M2 · Durable state

Remember what changed, what was observed, and what must happen next.

M3 · Execution evidence

Preserve provenance, integrity, timestamps, checks, and output linkage without overstating truth.

Open core + commercial factory

Keep the standard boring. Charge for useful execution.

CommandLayer's open components remain useful as reference schemas, cryptographic receipt primitives, verification rules, runtimes, and SDK surfaces. The commercial factory is where pricing, metering, provider composition, state, distribution, and hosted outcomes live.

Commons / open surfaces

Schemas, receipt formats, verification, reference runtime behavior, and SDK primitives should remain understandable and independently inspectable.

Commercial factory

One production control plane owns service manifests, routing, economics, adapters, execution state, telemetry, and outcome delivery.

Identity

Readable names are a layer, not a religion.

A machine service can simultaneously have an HTTPS endpoint, wallet, ENS name, ERC-8004 identity, platform ID, or future identifier. Premium functional names can be valuable public service identities without requiring ENS to become universal.

Design principle: identity, discovery, settlement, model providers, and directories stay replaceable. The service contract and evidence boundary stay stable.
